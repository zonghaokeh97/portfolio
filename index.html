My story

Curious about everything, from how machines work to how markets move.

I grew up in Melaka, Malaysia, the town where the country's history began. As a kid, when my parents wouldn't buy me toy cars, I built my own. In high school, the frustrations of dorm life led me to design and build a portable manual washing machine.

Cliché as it sounds, one night I dreamt I was walking through an old, Harry Potter-like building in England. I woke up knowing I had to study abroad, specifically at Cambridge, where my childhood idol Isaac Newton once studied. A PETRONAS scholarship covered my studies and made a dream I once thought impossible come true.

After graduating, I took the most unusual job I could imagine: working offshore on a full-scale LNG plant in the middle of the sea, handling machinery that looked like alien technology and learning to respond to real emergencies. They were the most transformative five years of my life.

Over those years, I grew curious about business, which brought me to Berkeley Haas. I still love to build. These days that means small projects that make daily life easier and help me learn faster, and I share them here for anyone to try.

If you're a prospective student or recent graduate curious about my path, I'm always happy to help. Feel free to reach out by email or LinkedIn.
